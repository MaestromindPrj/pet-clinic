INFECTIOUS DISEASE AWARENESS

When They Need Us Most.

Understanding the severe health challenges faced by street and rescue animals is the first step toward saving them. Early diagnostic care and strict isolation protocols make the difference between life and death.

• Canine Distemper (Hardpad Disease)

Distemper is a highly contagious, deadly viral disease affecting a dog's respiratory, gastrointestinal, and central nervous systems. Symptoms include severe ocular discharge and hyperkeratosis of the footpads (hardpad). Early medical isolation is absolutely crucial.

• Zoonotic Threat: Rabies Prevention

Rabies is 100% fatal once symptoms appear, but 100% preventable through timely canine vaccination. Protecting our community animals from Rabies is key to creating safe urban environments for both humans and street animals.
