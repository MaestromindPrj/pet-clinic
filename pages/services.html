Preventive Care

Protection starts before illness begins.

Vaccination for Dogs & Cats

Core and non-core vaccinations to safeguard against lethal infectious viruses.

  • DHLPP and Rabies vaccines for dogs
  • Feline Triple and Rabies for cats
  • Strict cold-chain storage integrity
  • Specialized street animal schedules

Deworming & Micro-Flora

Routine broad-spectrum parasite elimination to maintain absolute internal wellness.

  • Prevents zoonotic transmission to humans
  • Enhances gastrointestinal health
  • Safe pup & kitten liquid formulations
  • Dose tracking card provided

Advanced Tick Treatment

Eradicating tick infestations to block tick-borne blood parasitic fever.

  • Spot-on external therapy applications
  • Therapeutic chemical baths
  • Prevents Ehrlichiosis and Babesiosis
  • Surrounding environment management tips

Treatment & Specialized Care

When they need focused medical and therapeutic attention.

Skin & Dermatological Therapy

Targeted diagnostic swabs and microbial cultures to treat severe external lesions.

  • Sarcoptic & Demodectic Mange cures
  • Fungal (Ringworm) and bacterial treatments
  • Allergen mapping and diet guidance
  • Medicated medicament sprays

Surgery & Orthopedics

Complex soft-tissue and skeletal operations performed under strict sterile safety.

  • Spaying & Neutering sterilizations
  • Tumor and foreign body removals
  • Trauma fracture adjustments
  • Supervised ICU post-op wards

Diagnostics & Microbiology Lab

Accurate scientific analysis for effective clinical prescription.

Comprehensive Blood Tests

Complete blood counts (CBC) and chemical serum analysis to detect systemic illness.

  • Infection and anemia tracking
  • Organ-function profiles

Urinalysis Diagnostic

Investigating renal health, urinary tract infections, and diabetes metrics.

  • Microscopic sediment analysis
  • Protein-to-creatinine tracking

Pregnancy Diagnosis

Confirming gestation and preparing safe conditions for mother and litter.

  • Precise abdominal palpation
  • Ultrasonic verification

Diagnostic Ultrasonography

High-frequency soundwave mapping of internal organs and abdominal masses.

  • Non-invasive deep organ scans
  • Fluid collection checks

Histopathology for Tumors

Microscopic tissue biopsy assessment directed by our resident microbiologists.

  • Benign vs. malignant classification
  • Oncological staging support
